Concrete Pumping

Quick planning checklist, field tips, and official references for placing concrete by pump.

Planning & Pumpability

  • Trial the mix: Verify pumpability before the pour; paste volume and gradation are key.
  • Slump & cohesiveness: Match to line length/elevation; consider water-reducers or VMA as needed.
  • Lightweight mixes: Pre-wet aggregates per design to minimize slump loss/segregation.

Line Setup & Priming

  • Use appropriate line diameter; minimize reducers and tight bends.
  • Secure couplings; check gaskets and safety pins/clamps along the run.
  • Prime lines with approved materials; avoid water-only priming unless specified.

Pump Selection & Operations

  • Match output and pressure to placement rate and line resistance.
  • Maintain a steady rate; avoid frequent starts/stops to reduce blockages.
  • Coordinate discharge points with finishing/placement crew communication.

Safety & Troubleshooting

  • Establish an exclusion zone around the hopper, boom, and line.
  • Watch for air voids/slugging; reduce speed and re-prime if necessary.
  • Never open a line under pressure; follow lock-out procedures.
