Curing

Protect moisture and temperature so the mix meets strength and durability targets.

Methods

  • Moist curing: Wet burlap, soaker hoses, fogging, or curing blankets—keep continuously wet.
  • Curing compounds: Uniform spray to specified coverage; verify compatibility with flooring/sealers.
  • Membrane covers: Poly sheets sealed at edges for slabs where appropriate.

Time & Temperature

  • Maintain target temperature and moisture for the specified duration (often ≥7 days normal, longer for SCMs/low temp).
  • Use maturity or cylinder breaks to verify readiness for load/traffic/sawcuts as specified.
  • Protect against early drying—wind, sun, and low RH are the classic culprits.

Inspection & QA

  • Document method, start time, interruptions, and re-wetting cycles.
  • Check coverage rates for compounds; measure film where possible.
  • Coordinate with finishers and flooring trades early to avoid incompatibilities.

References

  • ACI 301 — Specifications for Concrete Construction (curing provisions)
  • ACI 305R — Hot Weather Concreting (related)
  • ACI 306R — Cold Weather Concreting (related)
