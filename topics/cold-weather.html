Cold-Weather Concreting

Prevent early-age freezing, maintain temperature, and achieve required strength.

Planning & Preheat

  • Define “cold weather” per spec and weather forecast; set up protection plan in pre-pour meeting.
  • Heat enclosures or ground as needed; remove snow/ice and standing water.
  • Use heated mix water/aggregates and/or appropriate accelerators when permitted.

Placement & Protection

  • Maintain concrete temperature on delivery and after placement within specified limits.
  • Insulate forms/slabs with blankets; avoid rapid cooling and thermal shock.
  • Prevent carbonation/CO exposure from heaters; vent combustion products.

Curing & Monitoring

  • Continue protection until the concrete reaches the specified strength/maturity.
  • Use sensors or frequent breaks to confirm—document temperatures throughout.
  • Adjust blanket thickness and heating based on section size and ambient swings.

References

  • ACI 306 — Cold Weather Concreting
  • ACI 301 — Specifications for Concrete Construction (cold-weather provisions)
  • ASTM C1064 (temperature), C31 (making/curing cylinders), C918 (admixtures) — as applicable
