Hot-Weather Concreting

Reduce rapid moisture loss, control set, and protect fresh surfaces in hot, dry, and/or windy conditions.

Plan & Monitor

  • Check forecast for temperature, wind, and relative humidity—calculate evaporation rate and plan mitigation.
  • Schedule pours early/late, add shade/wind breaks, pre-stage curing materials and finishing crew.
  • Pre-con meeting: finishing sequence, sawcut timing, curing method, and acceptance criteria.

Materials & Mix

  • Use chilled/tempered water or cooled aggregates; keep truck drums shaded when possible.
  • Water-reducers and set-retarders as specified; avoid on-site water addition—use admixtures instead.
  • Target temperature on delivery per spec; verify with ASTM C1064 measurements at site.

Placement & Finishing

  • Maintain a steady placement rate; minimize rehandling and delays.
  • Do not finish while bleed water is present; consider evaporation retardants per product data.
  • Coordinate with pump/placing crew so finishing starts on time without trapping water.

Curing & Protection

  • Start curing immediately after final finishing—moist curing preferred when feasible.
  • For compounds, apply at specified coverage rate; reapply if film is disturbed.
  • Protect from wind and sun during early hours; monitor surface moisture and temperature.

References

  • ACI 305R — Hot Weather Concreting
  • ACI 301 — Specifications for Concrete Construction (hot-weather provisions)
  • ASTM C1064 (temperature), evaporation rate guides/nomographs
