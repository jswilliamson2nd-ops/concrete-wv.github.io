Why it matters

West Virginia deserves a strong concrete network.

Concrete quality is built by people: the technician running tests, the inspector documenting acceptance, the producer controlling materials, the contractor placing the work, the engineer writing the requirements, and the student preparing to enter the industry.

This group gives those people a shared place to learn, connect, host events, support certification, and build a future ACI chapter that belongs to the whole state.

Do I need to be an ACI member to join?

No. Participation in the ACI West Virginia Interest Group is open to everyone — students, technicians, inspectors, contractors, producers, engineers, and educators.

ACI International membership is optional and may be useful for people interested in national committees, publications, and additional benefits.
